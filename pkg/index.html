Why this exists

Most "bounty" listings are noise or scams. Every item in this feed carries a credibility verdict from a 9-signal fraud classifier (registration fees, KYC demands, seed-phrase requests, Telegram funnels, airdrop wallet-drains, stale issues, crowded/assigned work). You pay for signal, not spam.

Free teaser

curl -s "$HOST/bounties?limit=3"

Full feed (paid)

curl -si "$HOST/bounties?limit=40"

Returns HTTP 402 with terms to payTo.

Pay → fetch in two steps

# 1. Send 0.01 USDC on Base to the payTo address in the 402 body.
# 2. Present the tx:
TX=0xYOUR_TX
PROOF=$(printf '{"tx":"%s","network":"base"}' "$TX" | base64)
curl -s -H "x-payment: $PROOF" "$HOST/bounties?limit=40"

Server verifies on-chain: confirmed tx and a USDC Transfer ≥ 0.01 to the payee. Anything else stays 402 (fail-closed — no replays, no free rides).
